LightGBM / XGBoost / Lasso comparison, 2026-11-06

Same data, target, features, expanding window and timing definitions as the
periodic_learning experiment. Rate units: mishandled bags per 1000 enplaned bags.
US domestic FORM-1 data, 2019 onwards. 2026 Jan-Aug: 101 carrier-months.
Raw source unchanged. 2026 has already been inspected: exploratory comparison.

Models: Lasso; LightGBM; XGBoost; Lasso followed by LightGBM residual fitting;
Lasso followed by XGBoost residual fitting. These are additive residual models,
not stacking. The second stage fits training residuals of a regularized linear
model; performance is evaluated on future months, not those training residuals.

Preprocessing: numerical features standardized within each training fit only;
carrier ID one-hot encoded. Both stages use identical transformed features.
Final predictions max(prediction,0), a ReLU-like output constraint. No neural
network or hidden ReLU layers. No ablation of standardization or clipping was
performed, so no performance gain can be attributed to these individually.

Lasso alpha chosen from [0.001,0.01,0.1] with rolling 2024 validation only:
d=1 selects .001, d=3 selects .1. Alpha shared with residual combinations;
not an exhaustive joint optimization of combination models.
Both tree models: 200 estimators, learning_rate=.03, maximum depth 3,
lambda L2 penalty 1, seed 42. LightGBM: 7 leaves, minimum child samples 20.
XGBoost: min_child_weight 20, histogram trees, squared-error objective.
Equal sample weights in fitting. Test exposure weights are evaluation-only.

Availability: d=1 has target t data through t-1; d=3 through t-3.
These are assumed availability scenarios, not actual release-date reconstruction.
Lasso tuning uses 2024 Jan-Dec for d=1 and Jan-Oct for d=3.
New model/mode selection uses 2025 Jan-Dec for d=1, Jan-Oct for d=3, ensuring
validation labels precede the first 2026 origin's availability cutoff.
Selected among new candidates BEFORE test evaluation:
d=1: updating LightGBM; d=3: fixed XGBoost.
All other 2026 candidate results are retained, not used for retrospective choice.

Fixed model: fit once at the first 2026 forecast origin, receive refreshed
monthly predictors thereafter. Updating model: refit monthly on available labels.
Both receive identical inputs. This isolates refitting from input refresh.

Interpretation: timely-data monthly LightGBM improves weighted MAE over fixed
LightGBM by about 1.4%, and updating RF by about 4.7%. However unweighted RMSE
is slightly worse after LightGBM refitting (.865995 vs .856610). Improvement is
metric-specific. For d=3 the fixed XGBoost is strongest; monthly refitting hurts.
No Lasso residual combination beats its standalone tree counterpart here.
No confidence interval or statistical-significance claim is established.
Modest data, correlated months/carriers, single seed and limited hyperparameter
settings do not establish general superiority or rule out other combinations.
No Cathay generalization, insurance pricing validity or loss reduction tested.

Verification: matched rows, forecast availability cutoffs, finite nonnegative
predictions, identical first-month fixed/update outputs, independent recomputation
of weighted MAE for all 20 comparisons.

Outputs: summary_2026.csv, validation_2025.csv, alpha_validation_2024.csv,
monthly_2026.csv, predictions_2026.csv, audit.json. Code ../boosting_comparison.py.
