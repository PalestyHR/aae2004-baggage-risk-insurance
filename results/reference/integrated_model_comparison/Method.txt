Integrated-data model comparison, 2026-11-06

Input: integrated_dataset/dataset/lag1/all_real.csv and feature_spec.json.
30 core features only. No synthetic rows, no optional incomplete NOAA weather.
Target: monthly mishandled bags per 1000 enplaned bags, not claims or loss rate.
Features use observations through preceding month; assumes timely internal data.
All models retrained each month on all strictly earlier target-month rows.
2020-2024 initial training; rolling validation 2025 (168 cases); rolling
exploratory test Jan-Aug 2026 (101 cases). 2026 was already inspected previously.
Historical revisions/publication dates are not reconstructed.

Same common preprocessing for learned models: carrier one-hot, remaining numeric
features standardized using current training fit only. Predictions clipped at zero.
All fitting rows equally weighted EXCEPT Poisson GLM: weights are historical
enplaned bags normalized by their training mean, matching a frequency/exposure
approach. Target-month actual exposures are NEVER inputs. Test exposures weight
the evaluation only. No tuning on 2026 and no broad hyperparameter search.

Predeclared settings:
ElasticNet alpha=.01, l1_ratio=.5, max_iter=20000.
PoissonRegressor alpha=.1, max_iter=2000, log link (sklearn default).
RandomForest and ExtraTrees: 300 trees, min_samples_leaf=8, seed42.
HistGBR: 200 iterations, learning_rate .03, 7 leaves, min_samples_leaf20,
L2=1, early_stopping=False, seed42.
LightGBM: 200 trees, learning_rate .03, max_depth3, 7 leaves,
min_child_samples20, L2=1, seed42.
XGBoost: 200 trees, learning_rate .03, max_depth3, min_child_weight20,
L2=1, squared-error loss, histogram trees, seed42.
Baselines: same month last year; 50/50 blend of preceding month and last year.

Model family is selected by 2025 volume-weighted MAE before scoring 2026.
All test results are retained; the test winner must not replace the validation
choice without acknowledging post-test selection. No statistical significance
or universal superiority claim. ARIMA/SARIMAX, Prophet and neural networks were
not trained in this comparison. Results do not establish Cathay transfer or
insurance pricing validity. Synthetic augmentation effects are not tested here.
